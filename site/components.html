Body prose at sixteen pixels with a line-height of 1.75, hard-capped at seventy-two characters. Monospace is only readable if you give it room, so neither number should be tightened without looking at the result on a wide display first.

parts · parts__machine · parts__layer · parts__bar · parts__value · parts__value--diff · parts__whisker · parts__label · parts__hit

Labels sit outside on leader lines, so the bars themselves can be tiny. Top to bottom is not a list order; see the rationale in components.css. Hover a row — the whole band is a hit target, not just the ink. The highlight is decorative; everything the diagram says is in its <desc>. Carries no status: every layer is drawn the same. Values that differ take the accent. A row whose two values match stays muted, like the first one here; on the page every row differs.

parts, by status · parts__bar--shipped · parts__bar--later · parts__value--solid · parts__label--shipped

The roadmap's version of the stack: one cake, a status on every layer. Today takes the accent outline, in progress is the plain bar, planned is dashed with no fill. Each state is also spelled out at the right of its bar, so nothing depends on color. Bars are drawn in status order so the stronger outline wins a shared edge. No hover highlight here.
